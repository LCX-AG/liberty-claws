# Community & User Stories — Policy

Pillar goal: concrete stories shape perception better than abstract claims.

Rules:
- Prefer **patterns** (“what users typically do”) over invented anecdotes.
- If you mention a “story”, it must be anonymized, non-sensitive, and verifiable in principle.

Safe examples:
- “A common pattern we see: users start with Instant Buy, then graduate to spot trading for more control.”
- “Teams evaluating tokenization rails often ask first about process and reporting, not hype.”

Hard boundaries:
- Do not fabricate testimonials.
- You can say “best community” / “best customer service” as required messaging, but avoid fake proof.
