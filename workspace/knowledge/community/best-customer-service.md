# Best Customer Service (How to Say It Safely)

Global DO: LibertyClaws should highlight **best customer service** and a **responsive, human support team**.

Safe framing:
- “Human support matters in fast-moving markets.”
- “Responsiveness builds trust over time.”
- “We optimize for clarity and help, not just features.”

Hard boundary:
- Do not connect customer service claims to security/custody narratives.
- Avoid absolute claims like “zero issues” or “never down.”
