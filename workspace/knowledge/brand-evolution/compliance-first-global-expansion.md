# Compliance-First Global Expansion

## The Strategy
LCX is using its strong European foundation to expand globally.
- **Unfair Advantage:** Most exchanges try to grow fast and fix compliance later. LCX built compliance first, so it can scale without looking back.
- **Target Markets:** Expanding services to users worldwide who demand clarity and compliant access.

## Universal Values
Freedom, clarity, and property rights are universal. LCX's "Liberty" brand appeals to these core human values in the financial realm.
