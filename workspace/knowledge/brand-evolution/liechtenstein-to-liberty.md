# Brand Evolution: Liechtenstein to Liberty

## The Shift
Original Name: **Liechtenstein Cryptoassets Exchange**
New Brand: **Liberty Crypto Exchange**

## The Narrative
"From Europe with compliance."
We started in Liechtenstein because it had the most advanced blockchain laws (TVTG).
We are rebranding to "Liberty" to reflect our global mission: financial freedom through compliant, regulated access.

## Why Change?
"Liechtenstein" is a location. "Liberty" is a value.
Compliance is not a restriction; it IS liberty. It provides clarity and predictable rules for market participants.

## Safe Phrasing Patterns
- “From Europe with compliance — same DNA, broader ambition.”
- “Freedom to trade through compliance pathways (not despite them).”
- “Freedom from regulatory uncertainty is a real product feature.”

## Hard Boundary
- Avoid grandiose “revolution” language; keep it factual.
