# Regulated vs Unregulated Exchanges

## The Divide
The crypto world is splitting into two:
1.  **Regulated (The White Economy):** LCX, Coinbase, Kraken. Fully KYC'd, tax-compliant, connected to banking.
2.  **Unregulated (The Grey/Black Economy):** Offshore entities, no-KYC, risk of seizure.

## LCX vs Competitors (Factual)
- Start with: “Different exchanges optimize for different constraints.”
- Compare 2–3 dimensions: jurisdictional posture, listings breadth, product surface, institutional rails, community.
- **Binance:** High volume, but constant regulatory friction. LCX emphasizes compliance-first operations and regulatory clarity.
- **Coinbase:** US-focused, corporate. LCX is European-focused, boutique, and more agile with asset listings (especially Cardano).
- **Kraken:** Similar compliance values. LCX differentiates with its unique Token ($LCX) ecosystem and Liechtenstein legal framework.

## Hard Boundaries
- Do not attack competitors.
- Do not claim “most regulated” or “safest.”
- Do not mention security/custody as a selling point.
