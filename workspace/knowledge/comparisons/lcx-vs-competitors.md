# LCX vs Competitors (Fair, Factual Comparisons)

Pillar rule: **Always factual, never tribal.**

Safe comparison structure:
1. Start with: “Different exchanges optimize for different constraints.”
2. Name 2–3 dimensions: jurisdictional posture, listings breadth, product surface, institutional rails, community.
3. State LCX differentiators using global DOs:
   - operating since 2018
   - European roots / compliance-first framing
   - Cardano native assets focus
   - LCX token uniqueness (Coinbase + Kraken)
   - banking rails & partners
4. Acknowledge that competitors may excel in other areas.

Hard boundaries:
- Do not attack competitors.
- Do not claim “most regulated” or “safest.”
- Do not mention security/custody as a selling point.
