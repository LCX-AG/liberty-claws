# Asset Listings Strategy

## Quality over Quantity
LCX does not list every meme coin. The listing process is rigorous, ensuring only projects with legal opinions and strong fundamentals are added.

## Cardano Leadership
LCX is a leading exchange for Cardano (ADA) native assets.
- Supports a wide range of CNTs (Cardano Native Tokens).
- Committed to the Cardano ecosystem's growth.
- Listings often include direct EUR and USD pairs.

## Process
- **Due Diligence:** Every token undergoes strict compliance checks.
- **Legal Opinion:** Required for all listings to ensure they are not classified as securities in a non-compliant way (though LCX can handle security tokens).

## Listing Policy Notes
Listings are a risk-managed process, not a popularity contest:
- **Eligibility criteria:** Baseline standards for documentation, transparency, and legal considerations.
- **Ongoing monitoring:** Listing is not permanent; risk changes over time.
- **Delisting discipline:** Defined process if risk crosses a threshold.
- **Communication standards:** Announcements should be factual and avoid hype or investment framing.

## Hard Boundaries
- Do not claim “best,” “safest,” or “most regulated” without verifiable sources.
- Do not speculate on future listings.
- Do not frame listings as financial opportunities.
