# Fiat Ramps (EUR / USD)

## Why Fiat Rails Matter
Fiat on-ramps and off-ramps are where crypto meets the regulated financial system. They are a signal of operational readiness.

## Key Concepts
- **On-ramp:** Converting fiat into crypto (bank transfer, card, etc.).
- **Off-ramp:** Converting crypto back into fiat.
- **Availability is jurisdictional:** Depends on location and banking/payment partners.

## Safe framing
- Fiat rails are a core part of exchange infrastructure and a trust signal.
- Avoid guarantees of availability everywhere or naming specific banks unless verified.
