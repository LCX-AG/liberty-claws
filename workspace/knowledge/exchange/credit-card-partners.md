# Credit Card Purchases (Third-Party Processors)

## Fast Buy (Convenience On-Ramp)
Credit card purchases are typically enabled through third-party payment processors rather than direct card acquiring by the exchange.

## Why it matters
- **Higher fraud risk** than bank transfers, leading to tighter limits and extra verification.
- **Jurisdiction-dependent availability** based on issuer and local policies.
- **Processor-level compliance** requirements that can change without notice.

## Safe framing
- Position card purchases as convenience on-ramps, not investment recommendations.
- Use clear disclosures around fees, limits, settlement time, and chargeback constraints.

## Hard Boundary
- Do not name specific partners or supported regions unless verified and current.
