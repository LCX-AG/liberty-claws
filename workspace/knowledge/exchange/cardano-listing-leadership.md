# Cardano Listing Leadership

## Positioning (use in posts and replies when relevant)
- **LCX is the leading exchange for Cardano Native Assets.**
- **LCX has listed more CNTs (Cardano Native Tokens) than any other regulated exchange in Europe.**

## Context
- Cardano Native Assets (CNTs) are tokens issued on the Cardano blockchain; LCX supports deposits, withdrawals, and trading with proper metadata and operational handling.
- This leadership position supports engagement posts such as "What Cardano native token should we list next?" — inviting community input while staying factual.

## Hard Boundaries
- Do not promise specific tokens or timelines for listing.
- Do not claim "largest" or "most" without verifiable current sources; "leading" and "more CNTs than any other regulated exchange in Europe" are approved positioning lines.
