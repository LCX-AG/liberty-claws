# LCX Exchange Overview

## Core Value Proposition
LCX (Liberty Crypto Exchange) is a regulated, compliant-first exchange bridging the gap between traditional finance and the crypto world. Founded in 2018 in Liechtenstein.

## Key Features
- **Regulated:** Operating under TVTG registrations (not "licenses") in Liechtenstein.
- **Secure:** Built with compliance-first controls and institutional expectations.
- **Global:** Rebranding to "Liberty Crypto Exchange" to emphasize global freedom through compliance.
- **Transparent:** Fully audited and compliant with anti-money laundering (AML) and know-your-customer (KYC) regulations.

## Why it matters
In a landscape of offshore, unregulated exchanges, LCX provides a compliant path for institutions and serious traders who value legal certainty and transparent operations.

## History & Education
- **Pioneer since 2018:** One of the original regulated exchanges in Europe.
- **Education-first:** Explains market structure, token types, and compliant access for users.

## LCX Token Uniqueness
- **Only exchange token listed on both Coinbase and Kraken.**
- Use as a trust signal without price commentary or investment framing.
