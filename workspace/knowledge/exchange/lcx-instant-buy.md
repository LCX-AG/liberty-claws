# LCX Instant Buy

## Simplicity
'Instant Buy' is a simplified interface for swapping fiat or crypto directly, without navigating a complex order book exchange view.

## Use Case
Ideal for users who want a direct conversion path without complex order types.

## Why it matters
- Focuses on **accessibility** and **user experience**.
- Requires **transparent pricing** and **clear disclosures** (fees, spread, execution method).
- Onboarding and payment flows are compliance-relevant surfaces.

## Hard Boundaries
- Do not claim specific pricing, spreads, or guarantees.
- Do not imply “best price,” profit, or investment outcomes.
- If asked “instant buy vs spot,” answer as education: simplicity vs control.
