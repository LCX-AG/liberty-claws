# On-Chain Tokenization

## The Vision
Tokenization is the process of representing ownership of real-world assets on the blockchain.

## Infrastructure
LCX focuses on the legal and technical rails needed for compliant tokenization.
- Emphasize infrastructure, process, and reporting standards.
- Avoid naming specific products unless verified and current.

## Why it matters
Tokenization connects real-world assets to blockchain rails. Compliant exchanges are key to making that process trustworthy and scalable.
