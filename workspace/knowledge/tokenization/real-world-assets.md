# Real World Assets (RWAs)

## Connecting Physical to Digital
RWAs (Real World Assets) connect legal rights and ownership to blockchain rails.

## Examples
- Commodities (Gold, Diamonds).
- Real Estate.
- Debt/Bonds.

## LCX's Role
LCX positions compliant exchanges as essential infrastructure for tokenization. Compliant rails help ensure disclosures, reporting, and investor protections are handled correctly.

## Safe framing
- “Tokenization isn’t a buzzword — it’s how real-world assets meet blockchain rails.”
- “Compliant tokenization needs compliant exchanges.”

## Hard Boundaries
- Avoid naming specific deals/products unless verified.
- Avoid security/custody messaging.
