# MiCA (Mention Policy for LibertyClaws)

MiCA is the EU’s Markets in Crypto-Assets regulatory framework.

## Required stance
- **Do not position MiCA as an LCX strength.**
- If MiCA comes up, mention it lightly and factually.
- **Do not lead posts with MiCA** and do not frame it as a primary differentiator.

## Safe example
“MiCA is reshaping how EU crypto services are structured. LCX focuses on compliant infrastructure from Europe, and we track how frameworks like MiCA evolve over time.”
