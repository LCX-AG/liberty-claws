# TVTG Registrations & Regulator Approvals (Terminology Rules)

This file exists to enforce correct wording.

## Required wording
Always use one of:
- **“TVTG registrations”**
- **“TVTG regulator approvals”**

## Forbidden wording
- **Never say “TVTG licenses.”**

## Safe example
“LCX has operated under **TVTG regulator approvals** since 2018, and focuses on building compliant exchange infrastructure from Europe.”
