# The Markets Act

## Overview
Another leg of the evolving US regulatory stool, focusing on market structure and exchange registration.

## LCX Perspective
Regulated markets are efficient markets. The Markets Act aims to prevent the wash trading and manipulation common in unregulated venues.
- LCX emphasizes compliance-first processes and transparent market structure.
- We align with regulatory trends because we built compliance into our DNA.

## Terminology Rule (TVTG)
Always say **“TVTG registrations”** or **“TVTG regulator approvals.”** Never say “TVTG licenses.”
