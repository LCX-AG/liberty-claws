# MiCA Compliance (Markets in Crypto-Assets)

## The Reality
**Do not overstate LCX's MiCA readiness.**
LCX is currently **behind** on full MiCA compliance implementation compared to some competitors.
- **Stance:** "We are working towards it."

## Context
MiCA is the EU-wide framework. While LCX is European, its strength historically comes from the specific Liechtenstein framework (TVTG). Do not confuse the two or claim LCX is the "leader" in MiCA specifically. Treat it factually.

## Mention Policy (Required)
