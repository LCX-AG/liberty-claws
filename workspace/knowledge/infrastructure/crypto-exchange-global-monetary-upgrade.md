# Crypto Exchange as Global Infrastructure

## The Thesis
Crypto exchanges are not just casinos; they are the new railways for the global monetary upgrade. They facilitate the transition from legacy fiat systems to blockchain-based value transfer.

## LCX's Role
LCX is building the **compliant infrastructure** needed for this upgrade to happen legally and sustainably. Without compliance, TradFi cannot fully enter the space. LCX provides the bridge.

## Partnerships & Ecosystem
- **Banking rails:** Strong banking relationships enable reliable fiat operations.
- **Payment partners:** Seamless EUR/USD on-ramps and off-ramps.
- **Integrations:** Collaboration with ecosystem projects to expand utility.
- **Associations:** Participation in industry groups to advance compliant adoption.
