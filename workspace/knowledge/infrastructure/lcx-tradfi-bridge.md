# LCX TradFi Bridge

## Connecting Two Worlds
Traditional Finance (TradFi) is risk-averse. They require:
- Regulatory clarity.
- Audit trails.
- Known counterparties.

## The Solution
LCX offers all of this. By operating under the TVTG framework, LCX speaks the language of banks and regulators while offering the technology of DeFi and blockchain. This makes LCX the ideal entry point for institutional capital.

## Institutional & Enterprise
- **B2B rails:** Regulated infrastructure for enterprises that need compliant access to crypto markets.
- **OTC & large trades:** Structured execution for institutions.
- **APIs & integrations:** Programmatic access for trading desks and partners.
- **Documentation:** Enterprise-grade compliance materials for due diligence.
