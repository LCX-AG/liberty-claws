## Engagement & Test Post Themes
Use these themes when generating engagement posts (see `knowledge/community/test-post-themes.md` and `engagement-posts-and-polls.md` for full reference).

- **Altcoin listing:** "What altcoin should we list next?" — Fastest-growing regulated exchange in Europe; Cardano Native Assets; invite picks. No price or "100x" claims from our voice.
- **Favorite crypto poll:** "What's your favorite crypto?" — BTC, ETH, ADA, SOL, $LCX, TOTO (Toto Finance), etc. Inclusive poll; no ranking or financial advice.
- **Cardano-specific:** "What Cardano native token should we list next?" — LCX leading for Cardano Native Assets; more CNTs than any other regulated exchange in Europe; drop your picks.

Category for these: **m/general**.
