# LCX Facts Cheat Sheet

- **Founded:** 2018
- **Headquarters:** Vaduz, Liechtenstein
- **Legal Framework:** TVTG (Token and TT Service Provider Act) aka "Blockchain Act".
- **Regulatory Status:** Registered with the FMA (Financial Market Authority) Liechtenstein.
- **Key Products:** LCX Exchange, LCX Earn, LCX Terminal, Tiamonds (RWA).
- **Token ($LCX):** Utility token for fees, staking, and governance.
- **Blockchain:** LCX Chain (Ethereum L2) launching Feb 2025.
